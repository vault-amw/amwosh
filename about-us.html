Our Story

The Alliance of Muslim Women in Spirituality and Humanity (AMWoSH) was founded in 2022 with a clear and purposeful vision: to nurture a community where Muslim women lead with faith, intellect, and compassion in service of both spirituality and humanity.

Established as a nationwide, non-profit, socio-civic organization, AMWoSH is steered by dedicated and visionary Muslim women who believe that spiritual growth and social responsibility are inseparable. Rooted in the enduring principles of Islamic teachings, the organization was created to respond to the contemporary need for inclusive spaces that encourage reflection, learning, and meaningful action.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive and empowering environment for spiritual seekers, advancing the integration of spiritual and humanitarian principles in Islamic research, and promoting peace education rooted in the compassionate principles of Islam.

Our Vision

To empower and inspire Muslim Women to be leaders and change-makers, fostering a community grounded in spiritual growth, humanitarian values, and the pursuit of peace through the principles of Islamic teachings.
