Our Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To create an inclusive and empowering environment for spiritual seekers, advancing the integration of spiritual and humanitarian principles in Islamic research, and promoting peace education rooted in the compassionate principles of Islam. In achieving Islam, Women and Peace, we use the three approaches:
  • Deliver a faith-based approach to conveying the message of Islam that aims to enrich their understanding of spiritual guidance;
  • Recognize the role of women as community buildersrooted from a service-oriented approach—and to provide them with the opportunities to be trailblazers in their communities and cooperate towards faith-inspired actions; and
  • To revive Islamic discourse among Muslim women through a research-centric approach, propelling intellectual growth and empowering them to lead discussions and inspire positive change.

Vision

To empower and inspire Muslim Women to be leaders and change-makers, fostering a community grounded in spiritual growth, humanitarian values, and the pursuit of peace through the principles of Islamic teachings.
