The Alliance of Muslim Women in Spirituality and Humanity (AMWoSH), established in 2022, is a nationwide non-profit, socio civic organization steered by dedicated and visionary Muslim Women that aims to cultivate an empowering and inclusive community for aspirants of spiritual enlightenment, to pioneer the integration of spiritual and humanitarian principles in Islamic research, and to promote peace education rooted in the principles of Islamic teachings. We aim to revive and invigorate Islamic discourse by fostering open dialogue, critical thinking, and scholarly inquiry among Muslim women and the broader community.
